# Landing page (index.html) — Persuade

Scope: the Waterwal home page, built first as a Claude Design canvas, then into index.html. Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: Utrecht homeowners/tenants wondering about cloudburst water at their door. Action: check your address (working field in the first viewport). Proof: official Klimaateffectatlas/FCAB result per address, bag count and price. Constraints: PRODUCT.md; no invented customers, reviews or hype words.

Approved comp: .impeccable/mocks/decision/canon.png (user picked category standard, then kept option one). Quality bar: user's references (Livo AI landing, Paulini travel landing).

## Direction contract
THESIS: A clear product page that answers "how high at my door" in one viewport; refuses alarmist storm imagery and data-dashboard clutter.
OWN-WORLD: White ground, deep logo green (#0a6a48 family) as the only brand colour, near-black green ink; one real-feeling photo of black swollen bags at a Dutch brick door; FCAB ramp only for risk; light water-splash texture at the edges; generous rounded fields.
STORY: Visitor understands the product (sand-free bags) and the check, believes it because the result is their own address from official data, and types their address.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav top (logo, links, green Wachtlijst). Left half: bold headline, one line, address field with full-width green button, three small notes. Right half: tall rounded photo of bags at a door with a floating result chip. Next section heading visible at the fold.
