# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Dutch homeowners and tenants (first: Utrecht and direct surroundings) who wonder whether a cloudburst could put water against their front door. They arrive curious or worried, often after news about heavy rain, and want a quick, honest answer for their own address.

## Product Purpose
Waterwal sells water-absorbing flood bags without sand. The site's main job is the address check: a visitor types an address and sees how high rainwater gets against their building in a heavy storm, then how many Waterwal bags they would need. Success = a visitor checks their address and understands their result; the bag advice and waitlist follow from it.

## Positioning
Address-level flood depth from official Dutch data (Klimaateffectatlas 2026, FCAB-omgevingsscore), joined to a concrete bag count for your own doors. The bag itself: a flat vacuum pack that lives on a shelf and swells into a dam in 3 to 4 minutes with fresh water, instead of 300 kg of sand.

## Operating Context
- Address search via PDOK Locatieserver; result per address from pre-built data files (Utrecht only for now).
- Two storms: 70 mm in one hour (about once per 100 years, official FCAB class) and 90 mm in one hour (about once per 250 years, computed with the FCAB method).
- FCAB classes and official colours: 0–2 cm white, 2–10 light pink, 10–15, 15–20 red, 20–30, >30 dark red. "Red" = building in the danger zone.
- Map with buildings in FCAB colours and water on the street.
- Bag advice: openings (front door, back door, garage, basement window, custom width), layers, bag count, €10 per bag.
- Waitlist sign-up (product not yet for sale).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static site on GitHub Pages (waterwal.eu), must also work opened from file://. Repo is public: no secrets.
- Only rainwater on the street, not rivers or sea (refer to overstroomik.nl). No guarantee; bags slow and turn water, they do not seal. Above roughly 50 cm, bags do not help.
- Bags: non-woven fabric, sodium polyacrylate filling, fresh water only, single use, non-toxic. Two versions: black with handles, white without; same price.
- Bag dimensions are still assumptions until measured.
- Language: Dutch.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Waterwal.
- Logo: green circular emblem (deep green, about #0a6a48) of a kneeling figure with a cap and clogs pressing a hand against a dike wall that holds back waves, with a small leak (the "boy at the dike" story). Lives in images/ when supplied.
- Voice: plain, honest Dutch, no hype; "Eerlijke antwoorden".

## Product Principles
1. The address answer comes first and must be readable in seconds.
2. Honest over alarming: show the official colour, say plainly what it means and what bags can and cannot do.
3. Make the alternative to sand tangible: small, stored, ready.
4. Every number traces back to the source data or a stated assumption.
